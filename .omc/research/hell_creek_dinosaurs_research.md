## Colour (no direct evidence for either species)
- Melanin evidence of **countershading** in the large armoured dinosaur *Borealopelta* → darker back, paler belly is a defensible choice for big herbivores. Exact colours are invented. Sources: [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/science/35033-alberta-nodosaur-dinosaur-color-royal-tyrrell), [Bristol PDF](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/ws/files/123285007/Full_text_PDF_final_published_version_.pdf)

## Hell Creek environment
- Low coastal plain with meandering rivers and moist soils; warm, frost-free (crocodilians present). Sources: [URI primer](https://digitalcommons.uri.edu/geo_facpubs/76), [UCMP](https://ucmp.berkeley.edu/museum/75th/ab35.html)
- Flora **angiosperm-dominated (>89% of leaf species)**: broadleaf flowering trees and shrubs; *Metasequoia* (dawn redwood) among conifers; ferns in the understorey. Sources: [GSA 2006](https://gsa.confex.com/gsa/2006AM/webprogram/Paper116412.html), [UND thesis](https://commons.und.edu/theses/101)
- → Scene: humid riverside forest of broadleaf trees and shrubs mixed with tall dawn redwoods, ferns low; **no grass lawns** (grasslands did not exist yet), no snow.
