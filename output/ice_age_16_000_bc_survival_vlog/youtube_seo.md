## Description
I'm an archaeologist. I've spent years digging up this camp — broken needles, painted skulls, mammoth bones stacked in zigzags. So I went back 18,000 years to see it working.

24 hours at Mezhyrich, central Ukraine, in the harshest phase of the last Ice Age. No tent, no gear, only what they use: frostnip first aid, why you never eat snow, why fat matters more than meat, bone fires, stone-boiling, fox-fur clothing, eyed bone needles — and what happens when a woolly mammoth mother decides you're too close.

Everything I explain is based on real archaeology. Where scientists still argue (were these huts homes? was that skull a drum?), I say so.
