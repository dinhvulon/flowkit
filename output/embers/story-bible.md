## 1. Premise & theme
- Spark: a firefighter holding a fire line hears on the radio that her little brother rode into the burn zone.
- Why it matters: everyone has protected someone by controlling them, and lost them that way.
- Theme word: **trust** (never changes; Nora's attitude to it does).
- What-if: what if the one person whose rule-following saved a town has to break her own rule — and trust the kid she never trusted — to save him?
- **Premise (Egri):** Love that tries to control everything drives away what it protects; love that trusts brings it home.
- **Controlling idea (McKee, idealistic):** A family survives when love trusts instead of commands — Nora saves Eli only when she stops giving orders and follows him.
- **Third track (Cron):**
  - Want: keep Eli safe and make the crew see her as solid, by doing everything by the book.
  - Misbelief: *"Going back is how people die. The line comes first, always."* — It was "true" once: their mother, a firefighter, went back against orders in a fire nine years ago and never came out.
  - Origin scene (backstory, shown only as one flash/prop): 14-year-old Nora at the station, holding her mother's scorched helmet.
- **Dramatic core (戏核):** the old mare **Juniper**, their mother's horse, which Nora has just sold to pay the bills. Eli runs into the smoke to save her; at the climax the horse is the one who knows the way out. Remove the horse and there is no film.
- Key prop (戏胆): mother's yellow helmet with "HALE" stencilled, now Nora's.
- Clock: red flag warning; the wind turns at 6 PM, the fire jumps the ridge; the only road out (Ridge Road) closes behind the line.
- Logline: *When a wildfire jumps the ridge, a by-the-book young firefighter must abandon the line she swore never to leave and go back into the smoke for the little brother who rode in to save their dead mother's horse — the horse she just sold.*
- Title: EMBERS.
